# Dashboard and account screens

Mode: Operate. Extend the established blue/green identity, with a code-first
build approved by the user. No replacement visual world.

The interface serves one task: open a personal Garmin history, sync it, and
inspect monthly pace. Use the existing navy, chart blue, green, white, and
quiet blue-gray surfaces, with a familiar single UI font and clear forms.

The top bar keeps the icon/name on the left and refresh/date on the right.
The green loading dot and a truthful record count remain visible while the
complete sync runs. Signed-out visitors see a focused two-field sign-in form;
signup adds Garmin email and password. Settings use an inline account screen.

The dashboard prioritizes year/month selection and the pace chart, with the
monthly table and cleaning detail below it. Avoid ornamental metric cards.
At 440px, all header actions have at least 44px tap targets, forms are one
column, and the data table stays legible without page overflow.

Direction contract: every required field and action is findable, account
data never mixes, errors give a next action, syncing remains visible across
all pages, and the last update changes only after complete success.
